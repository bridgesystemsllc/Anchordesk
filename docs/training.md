# Anchor Desk Training Guide

Training materials for KarEve customer service team.

## Overview

Anchor Desk is an AI-assisted customer service dashboard that:

- Pulls emails from five brand mailboxes into one queue
- Drafts replies using AI (you always send)
- Tracks tickets, customers, and orders
- Provides insights and reporting

## Key Concepts

### The Queue

- All tickets across all brands in one view
- Filter by status, brand, intent, or assignee
- SLA countdown ring shows response time remaining
- AI draft badge (sparkle) means a draft is waiting

### Ticket View

- Full conversation thread
- Customer info and order history
- AI-generated draft (edit before sending)
- Citations link to source materials

### Autonomy Level

Anchor Desk operates at L1–L2 autonomy:
- **AI drafts, human sends** — always
- No automated customer-facing messages
- All sends require explicit human action

## Daily Workflow

1. **Start of shift**
   - Check queue for urgent tickets (P1/P2)
   - Review any overnight escalations
   - Verify health in Settings > Mailboxes

2. **Working tickets**
   - Open ticket from queue
   - Review AI draft if available
   - Edit as needed, send reply
   - Close or escalate as appropriate

3. **End of shift**
   - Review any pending tickets
   - Hand off escalations properly
   - Check SLA status
